==============================================================================
PROBLEM 4 (a) -- conditional variance of x2 given x1
==============================================================================
Sigma_bar = Sigma_11 - Sigma_12 Sigma_22^-1 Sigma_21
          = var(x2) - cov(x1,x2)^2 / var(x1)
          = 4.104749 - 1.696902^2 / 1.099875
          = 1.486746
conditional sd = 1.219322   (unconditional sd of x2 = 2.026018)

factor by which the variance falls:
  Sigma_bar / Sigma_11 = 1 - Sigma_12^2 / (Sigma_11 Sigma_22) = 1 - rho^2
  = 1 - 0.798623^2 = 0.362201
the sd falls by sqrt of that = 0.601832

==============================================================================
PROBLEM 4 (b) -- does that factor depend on the observed x1?
==============================================================================
Sigma_bar = Sigma_11 - Sigma_12 Sigma_22^-1 Sigma_21 contains no 'a'.
Under the multivariate normal the conditional variance is therefore
constant in the conditioning value.  The term that settles it is the
absence of 'a': 'a' appears only in mu_bar, never in Sigma_bar.

==============================================================================
PROBLEM 4 (c) -- conditional mean of x2 given x1
==============================================================================
mu_bar = mu_1 + Sigma_12 Sigma_22^-1 (a - mu_2)
       = mean(x2) + [cov(x1,x2)/var(x1)] (x1 - mean(x1))
coefficient on x1: Sigma_12 Sigma_22^-1 = 1.696902 / 1.099875 = 1.542813
implied intercept                       = 0.071073

check against OLS of x2 on x1: slope 1.542813, intercept 0.071073
difference in slope: 4.441e-16
In regression terms the coefficient IS the OLS slope of x2 on x1:
cov(x,y)/var(x) is the same object arriving from a different door.

95% band: conditional mean +/- 1.96*1.219322 = +/- 2.389871
The half width does not depend on x1, so the band has constant width.
